Start here: the freelancer money system

The Gig Ledger exists because most money advice quietly assumes a biweekly paycheck. If your income arrives in clumps, you owe the IRS four times a year instead of through withholding, and nobody opened a 401(k) for you — so we built this site around the four moves that matter most:

  1. Split every payment. Move 25–30% of each payment into a separate tax savings account the day it arrives, before you spend a dollar.
  2. Pay quarterly with the safe harbor. Matching 100% of last year's tax (110% above $150k AGI) means underpayment penalties are never a question.
  3. Track deductions weekly. Fifteen minutes every Friday beats a panicked April weekend — and every $100 deduction saves roughly $37.
  4. Open a retirement account this month. A Solo 401(k) or SEP IRA shelters far more than a personal IRA. The best account is the one that exists.
